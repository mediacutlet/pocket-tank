Aqua Pets · Native screen review

Four moods. Your same aquarium.

Actual simulator renders of the application, at each device’s native resolution. Original preserves the existing artwork. Quiet Lagoon now uses the approved Living Lagoon direction: shaded naturalistic creatures, limestone ruins, veined plants and dark-glass menus. Tidepool Club retains its playful artwork. Blackwater (2026-10-10) is the realistic one: near-black water over dark sand, vivid lifelike creatures, a sunken Mayan pyramid and a fighter-plane wreck.

In Original, open Settings → SETTINGS / THEMES. In either modern theme, open Settings → Theme. Your choice is saved immediately. The naming screen keeps the seven-character roll mechanism. Creature sheets show all eleven native adult silhouettes. Jellyfish are available on Upgrades page 5 for 10 sand dollars. Ink and electricity previews animate the actual renderer. Idle creatures now occasionally visit the surface, puff, ink or spark; feeding, rest and interaction take priority.
