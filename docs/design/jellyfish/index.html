Meet the jellyfish

A softer rhythm
beneath the surface.

A gentle pulse, a translucent bell, a trail of drifting tentacles. Three interpretations, each made to belong in its aquarium.Approved design study. View the implemented jellyfish on native screens ↗

Designed to grow with you

Small, readable, recognisably jellyfish.

Proposed fry, adult and elder silhouettes at 1 CSS pixel per display pixel. These are a detail check; physical size depends on your screen. Longer elder tentacles retain the same compact bell.

Proposed movement

Pulse. Rise. Drift.

The bell contracts gently to rise, then opens as the jellyfish drifts. Tentacles follow with a slight delay. A touch would prompt a quicker pulse; rest would slow the rhythm. No ink or electric effects are proposed.

Design intent

One species. Three identities.

Original now follows the existing species’ flat palette, compact polygon shapes, white eye and tapered appendages. Quiet Lagoon takes its detail and dimensional lighting from Living Lagoon. Tidepool Club uses a warm scalloped bell and playful ribbon arms.

Aquarium views place the proposed adult design over actual native tank backgrounds. The jellyfish and motion are browser mockups, not firmware renders. The implemented native version is available in the theme gallery linked above.
