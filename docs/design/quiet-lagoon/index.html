Like a window into still water.

SCULPTURAL · TACTILE

Porcelain Cove

Pendant · 466 × 466 · scaled to fit

A miniature sanctuary in celadon.

A handmade underwater world. Creatures feel carved from glazed stone, plants become folded leaves, and a softly worn pavilion rests on ivory sand. Warm, quiet and easy to read at a glance.

Ivory / celadon / jade / clay
Creatures
Rounded ceramic volumes, engraved details and a single glaze highlight.
Habitat
A moon-gate pavilion, sculpted coral and clean, folded plant shapes.
Interface
Ivory trays, jade insets and an engraved battery with a pearl charge marker.

DESIGN RECOMMENDATION

Living Lagoon for immersion.
Porcelain Cove for quiet character.

I recommend Porcelain Cove for the pendant: its sculpted silhouettes and restrained detail should remain clearer at a small physical size. Living Lagoon is the richer, more realistic option if depth and creature detail matter most to you.

These are vector-based visual proposals with simulated depth, not a real-time 3D engine or firmware screenshots. The device views use the actual display aspect ratios and keep controls inside a conservative circular safe area. Final on-device detail and performance would be tuned after you choose a direction.
